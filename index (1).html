Questions we hear often

How much does CCTV installation cost?

A basic home system starts around ₹12,000. The price depends on cameras, cable length and storage. We give a fixed quote after a free site survey.

How long does installation take?

Most homes and shops finish in one day. Larger offices and factories take two to five days.

Can I watch cameras on my phone?

Yes. We set up the app and show you how to watch live video and replay footage.

Will it work at night or in a power cut?

Our cameras have night vision. We can add a UPS so recording continues in a power cut.

Which areas do you serve?

Chennai and nearby areas. See the list above or call us to check your location.

What does the warranty cover?

Cameras and recorder carry the brand warranty. You get the warranty card and GST invoice. Damage from water, power surges or misuse is usually not covered, and we explain this in your quote.

Are the products genuine?

Yes. We supply branded equipment with a serial number and invoice, so you can check it with the brand.

Can you reuse my old cables or cameras?

Often yes. During the survey we test your existing cables and tell you honestly what can stay, to save you money.

How long is the footage stored, and who can see it?

It depends on the hard disk size and camera count. We tell you the number of days in the quote. Only you hold the password to your system.

Is there a charge for the site survey?

No. The site survey and quote are free and carry no obligation.
